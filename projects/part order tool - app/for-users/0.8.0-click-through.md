# 0.8.0 click-through (mock mode)

For Brian, before the 0.8.0 build (Plan H task H6). 0.8.0 renames the app **SPI Parts Hub** and puts a launch page in
front: one card per area, and the left menu shows one area at a time. Mock mode uses made-up data and never writes to
SharePoint, so click freely. Everything resets when you reload the page.

**Start:** in PowerShell, `cd C:\Users\bfox\Documents\Git-Projects\nonjd-order-app`, then `npm run dev`, and open
the address it prints (usually http://localhost:5173). You're signed in as "Test User".

**The mock numbers:**
- **Branch 1:** 17 parts to order worth $835.03, 1 part that could come by transfer, 1 Equip minimum with no demand.
- **Branch 92:** 8 parts to order worth $146.00, 1 part that could come by transfer, 2 minimums with no demand.

Note anything that looks wrong, reads badly or feels slow next to the step number.
