# 0.15.0 try-it: Stock on superseded parts, JD and non-JD (mock mode)

For Brian, before deploying 0.15.0. Ben's request of 2026-11-05: parts with stock on hand but a **Super To**, so stock
still sitting under an old part number can be moved or relabelled. It's a built-in in two places: an **Inventory**
report for JD parts and a **Cleanup** check for non-JD parts. Same rule in both: on hand more than 0 and Super To not
blank. Any Super To counts, notes such as `BOLTS` too, so you can see and judge them.

**Start:** in PowerShell, `cd C:\Users\bfox\Documents\Git-Projects\nonjd-order-app`, then `npm run dev`, and open the
address it prints. Mock mode uses made-up data and never writes to SharePoint; a page refresh starts the mock over.

## 1. JD parts: Inventory

Open **Inventory**. The built-ins now end with **Stock on superseded parts**, and it counts at once (no Count button:
parts with no activity have no stock, so it never needs them): **2 parts**. Open it: **JD-SS-BOLTS** (Super To
`BOLTS`) and **JD-SS-OK** (Super To `R334455`), both at branch 7. The columns are Branch, Part, Description, On hand,
Cost, **On-hand value** (new), Super To, Source and SLC; click On-hand value to sort by it. Not listed: JD-SS-NONE (a
Super To but nothing on hand) and JD-SS-BLANK (a Super To of only spaces, which counts as blank). **Export CSV**: the
headings are `Branch,PartNumber,Description,OnHand,Cost,OnHandValue,SuperTo,Source,SLC`.

*Look for:* whether listing the `BOLTS`-style notes is useful or noise; whether the columns are the ones Ben needs.

## 2. Non-JD parts: Cleanup

Open **Cleanup**. Three built-ins now: Minimums with no demand, Stock value with no quantity and **Stock on
superseded parts**: **1 part**. Open it: **MC-SS-1** (Old filter head, branch 1, on hand 3, Super To MC-SS-2), total
**$54.00**. MC-SS-2's other half, MC-SS-NO, has a Super To but no stock, so it isn't listed.

## 3. Super To in your own checks

**Cleanup → New check**: **Super To** is a new field (is, is not, is blank, is not blank and so on), so a saved check
can use it, for example "Super To is not blank" with "On hand is more than 5".

**Then:** App Settings → About should say **Version 0.15.0**. (On mock, MC-SS-1 also appears in the Scrap default, as
a part with stock and no sales would.) In the real app expect about **2,477** JD records at 20 branches and **698**
non-JD records at 19 branches (Prod, 2026-11-05).
