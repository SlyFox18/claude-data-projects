# 0.15.1 try-it: see a part without leaving your work (mock mode)

For Brian and Ben, before deploying 0.15.1. Ben's request of 2026-11-05: on an order in progress, check a part's details
and come back without losing anything. Now clicking a part number opens a **panel** over the right of the screen,
look only. The page stays exactly as it was underneath.

**Start:** in PowerShell, `cd C:\Users\bfox\Documents\Git-Projects\nonjd-order-app`, then `npm run dev`, and open the
address it prints. Mock mode uses made-up data and never writes to SharePoint; a page refresh starts the mock over. If
`npm run dev` was already running, stop it (Ctrl+C), start it again and press Ctrl+Shift+R in the browser.

## 1. Ben's example

1. **Non-JD ordering → Recommended Reorder.** Pick **Several branches…**, choose **1** and **92** (the mock has only a
   couple of transfers into branch 1 alone), and go to step **1, Transfers**.
2. Tick **3** transfers. The Save button says "Save 3 transfers".
3. Click a **part number**. The panel opens with the part's status, **Detail** and **History** tabs and **Other
   branches**. There's no "Leave this page?" question. There's also no Settings tab: that's on purpose.
4. Click **History** to see the sales chart.
5. Press **Close** (or Esc, or click the dimmed page on the left). You're back with **3 still ticked**.

*Look for:* is the panel wide enough for the chart and Other branches? Do you miss Settings here?

## 2. Inside the panel

- **Other branches:** click another branch's name. The panel switches to that branch; you don't leave the page.
- **Open full page** (top of the panel): the panel closes and, because you have ticks, **"Leave this page?"** appears,
  as it always has. **Stay** keeps your ticks. Leave goes to the full part page, which still has Settings.
- **A new tab:** right-click a part number → Open link in new tab, or Ctrl-click it: the full part page opens in a new
  tab and your page stays put.

## 3. Everywhere else

Click a part number on each of these; each opens the same panel:
- **One-Time Order** (a part on the review list) and an order on **Orders** (a line of a saved order).
- **Transfers** and a **transfer order's** lines.
- **Returns / Scrap**: the lines of a document, and the parts in the New return wizard.
- **Cleanup**: any check's results, such as Stock on superseded parts.
- **Part Lookup** (clicking anywhere on the row does it too) and **Special statuses**.

**Inventory reports are unchanged:** JD parts have no part page, so those reports keep their own record panels.

**Then:** App Settings → About should say **Version 0.15.1**.

One thing to know: the panel reads the live settings when it opens, so if someone changed a setting since your page
loaded, the panel can show a newer number than the page beneath (it shows a "Changed" tag when it differs from this
morning's run). The browser's Back button leaves the page rather than closing the panel.
