# 0.13.0 try-it: Border Stores and 2 & 4 Cotton (mock mode)

For Brian, before deploying 0.13.0. Inventory gets two more built-ins that compare a part at one branch with the same
part at the other branches: **Border Stores** (branches 95, 91, 96) and **2 & 4 Cotton** (cotton parts at branches 2
and 4, including parts the branch carries but has never stocked or sold). Copies can now say which other branches
count ("Count other branches where"), use "any of" groups, and include parts with no activity.

**Start:** in PowerShell, `cd C:\Users\bfox\Documents\Git-Projects\nonjd-order-app`, then `npm run dev`, and open the
address it prints. Mock mode uses made-up data and never writes to SharePoint; a page refresh starts the mock over.

## 1. Open the two new reports

Open **Inventory**. The list has three built-ins: MLPF Gaps (1 part), **Border Stores** (1 part) and **2 & 4 Cotton**,
which shows a **Count (loads more data)** button instead of a number (it needs the parts with no activity, which
aren't loaded until someone asks). Open **Border Stores**: it finds **JD-BS-OK** at branch 95, with **Other-branch demands 10** and
**Other branches with demand 5** (the thresholds exactly). Click the part number to see both figures in the panel.
**Export CSV**: the last three headings are Demands, OtherBranchDemands, OtherLocationCount, as in the workbook.
Back on the list, press **Count** for 2 & 4 Cotton: it finds 2 parts. Open it: **JD-CT-OK** at 4 (coded COTTON) and
**JD-CT-770** at 2 (coded COMBINE there, but 770 at another branch, so it counts as cotton).

*Look for:* whether "Other-branch demands" and "Other branches with demand" read clearly next to the workbook's
OtherBranchDemands / OtherLocationCount, and whether the Count button makes sense.

## 2. Make your own Border Stores

On Border Stores, **Copy as my own**. The editor has two lists: **Rows to show** (which branch rows the report
lists) and **Count other branches where** (which rows at the other branches count towards the two figures; here,
every branch except 2 and 4, with the six part filters). In **Rows to show**, change the branch condition to **one of
7, 14** and **Other-branch demands** to **≥ 8** (both near the top of the list; the pool reads "Branch not one of 2,
4"). **Try it** now finds JD-BS-B7 at branch 7. Name it, **Save
report**: "Report saved.". Then **Edit**, turn on **Include parts with no activity**, and save again ("Report
updated.").

*Look for:* whether the two lists are easy to tell apart, and whether you'd know which one to change to compare
different branches.

## 3. Try an "any of" group

In any copy (or a new report), **Add 'any of' group**: a box "Any of these" where only one line has to match, for
example **Dealer group is one of COTTON** *or* **Dealer group at any branch is one of 770**. Cleanup checks have the
same button. A group needs 2 to 10 lines; **Make it a plain condition** turns a one-line group back into a condition.

*Look for:* whether "any of" is clear next to the main list's "all must match".

**Then:** App Settings → About should say **Version 0.13.0**. In the real app, until the first v7 publish, 2 & 4
Cotton says "Parts with no activity aren't in today's data yet"; that's expected for the first morning. Pressing
Count in the real app loads about 11 MB the first time in a session.
