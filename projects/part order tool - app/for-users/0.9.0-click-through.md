# 0.9.0 click-through (mock mode)

For Brian, before deploying 0.9.0. New: the **Returns & scrap** area. Return and scrap rules per franchise, a wizard
that picks the qualifying parts (pre-picked up to the allowance for a return; Transfer instead where another branch
has demand), saved return / scrap documents with their own status flow, and **On return / On scrap** tags wherever a
part's stock is held by an open document. Mock mode uses made-up data and never writes to SharePoint.

**Start:** in PowerShell, `cd C:\Users\bfox\Documents\Git-Projects\nonjd-order-app`, then `npm run dev`, and open
the address it prints (usually http://localhost:5173). The mock data lives in the browser tab, so don't refresh the
page part-way through; a refresh starts the mock over.

**The mock parts:** franchise MM at branch 1 has three parts that qualify for the "MM annual return" rule:
**MM-RET-DEM** (also wanted at 92 and 95), **MM-RET-OLD** and **MM-RET-HELD** (2 of its 5 are already on an open Draft
return, MM-RET-20261020-1400). For scrap, franchise AM has **AM-SURP-1** (at 92, wanted at branch 1) and **AM-BELT-2**.

| # | Do | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | On the launch page, find the **Returns & scrap** card. | "Vendor returns and scrap, by franchise rule." and **1 open return**. |
| 2 | Click it. | The **Returns** list, with one Draft (MM-RET-20261020-1400) and a **New return** button. The left rail has **Returns**, **Scrap** and **Return & scrap rules**. |

| 4 | Click **New return rule**, name it **TEST**, franchise **MM**, then **Save rule**. | "Rule 'MM annual return' is active for MM. Make this one active instead?" Click **Stay**, then **Close** and **Discard**. Nothing changes; one active rule per franchise. |
| 5 | Optional: click **Edit** on MM annual return and use **Try it**. | 3 parts, $326.00, allowance $433.04, credit $277.10. Close without saving. |
| 6 | **Returns → New return.** | Steps **Set up · Parts · Review & save**. Franchise **MM**, the rule and its terms, **Demand window (months)** 18. |
| 7 | Click **Choose parts**. | MM-RET-DEM, MM-RET-OLD and MM-RET-HELD, all ticked. Meter **$326.00 of $433.04 allowance**; footer **3 lines · $326.00 · credit $277.10**. MM-RET-HELD offers only 3 (2 are on the old Draft). |

| 12 | Type **RA-7781** in **RA number** and press Enter. | "RA number saved." |
