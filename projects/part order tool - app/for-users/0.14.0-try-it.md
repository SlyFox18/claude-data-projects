# 0.14.0 try-it: Corporate Stocking, one row per part (mock mode)

For Brian, before deploying 0.14.0. Inventory gets its fourth built-in, **Corporate Stocking** (from the SPI Corparate
Stocking workbook): parts with nothing on hand or on order at any branch (except 2 and 4) that had a Corp demand at
3 or more branches. Unlike the other reports it lists **one row per part**, not one per branch, and copies can change
which records count, what they must be, where demand counts and the thresholds.

**Start:** in PowerShell, `cd C:\Users\bfox\Documents\Git-Projects\nonjd-order-app`, then `npm run dev`, and open the
address it prints. Mock mode uses made-up data and never writes to SharePoint; a page refresh starts the mock over.

## 1. Open Corporate Stocking

Open **Inventory**. The built-ins now end with **Corporate Stocking**, with a **Count** button (it needs the parts with
no activity). Press it: **4 parts**. Open it:

| Part | R18 demands | Branches with demand | Locations with demand |
|---|---|---|---|
| JD-CS-OK | 7 | 4 | 1, 7, 14, 93 |
| JD-CS-AN | 6 | 3 | 1, 7, 93 |
| JD-CS-B2 | 3 | 3 | 1, 7, 14 |
| JD-CS-SLC | 3 | 3 | 1, 7, 14 |

There's no Branch column; the branch filter is **Demand at branch** instead. Pick **93**: only JD-CS-OK and JD-CS-AN.
Back to All branches, click **JD-CS-AN**: the panel lists every branch's record. 1 and 7 say **Counted: Yes**; 93 says
**No: not in Records that count** (its source is AN), but its 4 demands still count ✓, which is why it reaches three
locations, exactly as the workbook does. **Export CSV**: the headings are the workbook's, ending R18 Demands, Count of
Locations with Demands, Locations with Demands.

*Look for:* whether the panel explains a part clearly, and whether "Demand at branch" is what you'd want instead of a
branch filter.

## 2. Make your own

On Corporate Stocking, **Copy as my own**. The editor has four cards: **Records that count** (branch not one of 2, 4
and the four part filters), **Every counted record must** (on hand 0, on order 0, Super To blank), **Demand counts
where** (branch not one of 2, 4) and **Parts to show** (Branches with demand ≥ 3). Change Parts to show to **≥ 4**:
**Try it** finds 1 part (JD-CS-OK). Name it and **Save report**; the list shows it with 1 part.

*Look for:* whether the four cards are easy to tell apart, and whether you'd know which one to change to, say, stop
counting branch 12.

## 3. A new report from scratch

The Inventory page has no New report button (reports start as a copy), so type the address: the one `npm run dev`
printed with `/inventory/new` on the end. The page has a new **Report type** choice, **One row per branch** (as before) or **One row per part**.
Choose one row per part: the first three cards start empty ("Every record counts." and so on) and Parts to show starts
with Branches with demand ≥ 1; Try it finds 41 parts (the first 20 listed). Leave without saving: it asks first.

**Then:** App Settings → About should say **Version 0.14.0**. In the real app Corporate Stocking loads the parts with no
activity (about 11 MB the first time in a session), like 2 & 4 Cotton. A saved one-row-per-part report can't be read
by 0.13.x, so 0.14.0 goes to Fedora before anyone saves one.
