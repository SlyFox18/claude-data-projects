For Brian, before deploying 0.16.0. Ben's SPI Pieces In Set workbook as **three new, separate rows in the Inventory
reports list**. Nothing else changed: the five reports already there find the same parts and export the same CSVs, and
**nothing appears on the order screens**. The Reason Codes work (F12) comes after this.

**Start:** in PowerShell, `cd C:\Users\bfox\Documents\Git-Projects\nonjd-order-app`, then `npm run dev`, and open the
address it prints. Mock mode uses made-up data and never writes to SharePoint; a page refresh starts the mock over.

## 1. The list

On the launch page click **Inventory management**. You land on **Reports**, the list titled **Inventory reports**.
Scroll down past the first five built-ins (MLPF Gaps, Border Stores, 2 & 4 Cotton, Corporate Stocking, Stock on
superseded parts). Rows six to eight are the new ones, each marked **Built in**:

| Row | The Parts column says |
|---|---|
| **Pieces In Set** | 7 parts |
| **Pieces In Set: 3 Months or Less** | 3 parts |
| **Pieces In Set Review** | 1 part |

All three count at once, with no Count button.

## 2. Pieces In Set

In the **Pieces In Set** row click **Open**. There are 7 parts, all at branch 7. The table is wide, so scroll it sideways
to see every column. Check these:

| JD-PIS-OVER | Round up, Recommended qty, Coverage flag | 8, 1, Overstocked (it stays on the list, R-D5) |

Click **Export CSV**. The headings are the workbook's: `Branch,Part #,Description,Source,SLC,Commodity Code,Dealer Group

Class,Stock Position,Months of Supply,Coverage Flag`.

## 3. The details panel

Still on Pieces In Set, click the part number **JD-PIS-OK**. The panel on the right now lists the new fields below Sales 24
months: Bin qty, InMaster PIS, SPI PIS, Match %, PIS multiple %, Invoices, Sales qty, Stock position, Round up, Recommended

The same panel on any JD part in any Inventory report shows these lines too. On a part with no invoices, Invoices and SPI
PIS say 0 and the calculated lines say —.

## 4. The other two lists

Click **← Inventory reports**. In the **Pieces In Set: 3 Months or Less** row click **Open**: JD-PIS-ACT, JD-PIS-OK and
JD-PIS-ZERO. JD-PIS-OVER is not there, because it has more than 3 months of supply.

Click **← Inventory reports**. In the **Pieces In Set Review** row click **Open**: one part, JD-PIS-REV, InMaster PIS 4,
SPI PIS 1, Match % 50, PIS multiple % 25. In its CSV the SPI PIS column is headed `Most Common Qty`, as in the workbook.

## 5. The Buy List, as your own copy

The workbook's Buy List isn't a built-in. This is how to make it.

1. Click **← Inventory reports**. In the **Pieces In Set** row click **Copy as my own**.
2. Click **Add condition**. On the new row (Condition 6): Field **Tier**, comparison **one of**, then type `1 - Act Now`
   and press Enter, type `2 - Likely` and press Enter.
3. Click **Add condition**. On the new row (Condition 7): Field **PIS class**, comparison **is not**, value
   `Box / Bulk Qty`.
4. Click **Add condition**. On the new row (Condition 8): Field **Recommended qty**, comparison **>**, value `0`.
5. **Try it**, at the bottom of the page, should say **2 parts**: JD-PIS-ACT and JD-PIS-OVER.
6. In **Name** type `Buy List`, then click **Save report**. Its results page opens, and it's on the list under the
   built-ins.

*Look for:* whether JD-PIS-OVER belongs on a buy list. It's overstocked but still recommends 1 to reach a full set, as
Excel does. A copy can leave those out by adding Coverage flag is not Overstocked.
