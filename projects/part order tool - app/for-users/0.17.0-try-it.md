# 0.17.0 try-it: JD ordering (mock mode)

For Brian, Monday, before anything is released. This is **a new area, JD ordering**, with its own Home, Recommended Reorder,
One-Time Order, Templates, Orders, Rules and Special statuses. It ends in an exported list, beside Prism, and doesn't connect
to Prism or JD's ordering system.

**Start:** in PowerShell, `cd C:\Users\bfox\Documents\Git-Projects\nonjd-order-app`, then `npm run dev`, and open the address
it prints. Mock mode uses made-up data (part numbers starting `JD-ORD-`) and never writes to SharePoint. **A page refresh
starts the mock over**, so anything you saved disappears. Don't refresh until step 8 says to. The orange "stale" badge is
normal in mock mode, because the mock's data is dated Oct 21.

**What's separate from non-JD:** its data files, its rules list, its orders and its templates. **What's shared:** the part
settings list. A JD setting has franchise D, and each Special statuses page shows only its own.

## 4. Branch 12, a saved order and its CSV

3. Click the part number **JD-ORD-12A**. The details panel opens on the right. **Why this number** says: *Default: sold 12 in 12
   months (1 a month) x 4 weeks of supply gives a target of 1. On hand 0, on order 0, so order 1.* Click the **History** tab: Monthly

   selected**.
4. Click **Create order (2)**. The review says **Saves 1 order (one per franchise) with 2 lines, $134.60 at cost, as Draft.**
5. Click **Save order (2 lines)**. The order opens. Its name starts `12-D-`, and it says **Draft**, **Branch 12**, **Franchise D**.
6. Click **Export CSV**. A CSV downloads with both parts, in the same format as a non-JD order. The status changes to **Exported**.
7. In the left nav click **Orders**. There is **1 order**, the `12-D-` one.

## 8. Rules, last (this step changes numbers)

1. In the left nav click **Rules**. The first line names the **JD_OrderRules** list. Two rows, both **12 months, 3 demands, 4 weeks**:
   **Default** and **Built-in default** (the fallback for a part no rule fits). Non-JD's built-in default says 12 weeks, and it hasn't changed.
2. On the **Default** row click **Edit**. Scroll down to **Try it**, which is on Branch 1.
3. Change **Supply** from 4 to `12`. After a moment **Total value at cost** says **$146.20** before and **$454.80** after, a change of
   **+$308.60**. **Order lines** stays at 3. Nothing is saved yet.
4. Click **Save rule**.
5. Click **Recommended Reorder**, on Branch 1. JD-ORD-REO now says **+8 ($304.80)** and JD-ORD-OOS **+6 ($120.00)**, each with a
   **Changed** label, because they differ from this morning's run. The list total is **$454.80**. In **Non-JD ordering** nothing moved:
   the launch page's Non-JD card still says **$835.03**.
6. Refresh the page to put everything back.

## Left out of this release

- **Transfers:** no JD transfers of any kind.
- **Spiking, EOQ and the check on Prism's Pre-Approved orders:** these wait for Ben's criteria, so lists won't match Prism exactly.
- **Rules with tags (F12) and the combine vault (F13):** these come later.

**Non-JD is unchanged** apart from two things: its Special statuses page now leaves out franchise D settings, and its orders and
templates lists read only rows with a blank Area, which is every row they have today. The **Area** box gains a JD ordering entry.

**Not something you can see here:** on a publish before contract 9, the card says Coming later and the `/jd` pages say "JD ordering
isn't in today's data yet. It arrives with the next publish." Automated tests cover this, but mock mode doesn't show it.

**Dev check results:** this note covers the screens on mock data only. The results of the JD build in Dev come separately, from the
data work.
